Admin guide

Shared openly so everyone can see how reviews are run. Only admins can sign in to the admin pages.

Everything your director does to run a review, from first setup to the last letter. Print it and tick the boxes as you go, or keep it open next to the admin pages.

Watch the 90 second admin video Go to admin

The one thing to know first: how messages reach people. Every message to staff (their link, a reminder, and "your letter is ready") has an Email everyone button. It emails each person who has an email address and Send email checked in People, and tells you how many it skipped. For those few, press Copy and send it yourself by text. If Settings, Automated Emails says email is not set up yet, you copy and send every message yourself.

1. Once, before your first review about 20 minutes

  • Sign in. Go to /admin/ and type your email. You get a code by email. Type it in and you stay signed in for the day. Only emails on the admin list get in.
  • Settings, Organization. Check your organization's name, what staff call you, and your logo. Staff see these on every screen, and letter drafts are written in your voice.
  • Values. Make sure your values and their descriptions are right. The AI measures each letter against them.
  • People. Add everyone who will write or receive reviews: staff, interns, volunteers, board. Paste a list to add many at once. Set Group to say which. Add each person's email, and leave Send email checked for anyone the app should email. Uncheck it for anyone who would rather hear from you directly.
  • People. Set Role to Director for yourself, with your email. Replies to the app's emails come to you. Set Team lead for anyone who leads a group. Leads read their group's shared letters.
  • Settings, Language. Leave Offer ʻŌlelo Hawaiʻi off until the screens are translated. A fluent speaker can fill in every screen text under Manage translations, and it goes live right away. Then turn it on.
  • Settings, AI. Choose whether to use AI. On: staff can answer by voice, and AI compiles a first draft of each letter and report for you to rewrite. Off: staff type, letters start from the answers word for word, reports start from an outline, and nothing goes to any AI model. Everything else works the same. With AI on, you can paste an Anthropic key there for warmer, more specific drafts, about 25 cents a review. The page shows how to get one.
  • Settings, Sample review. Optional. Load the sample to see a finished letter and a reflection, then remove it.

2. Set up a review about 30 minutes

  • Cycles. Start a new review. Give it a name (like Hoʻoilo 2026), an open date, a close date two or three weeks later, and your values. A Next step banner on the review tells you what to do from here.
  • People tab. Everyone active starts checked. Uncheck anyone sitting this one out. Add anyone new right there.
  • Questions tab. Read every question. Peer reviews start with mahalo. Story questions ("tell me about a time") get the best answers. Mark 2 or 3 per group as five minute questions for people short on time.
  • Setup tab. Optional. Check Include an upward review to have everyone review you, and each group review its team lead.
  • Reviewers tab. Press Auto fill, then change anything you like. Aim for 2 or 3 reviewers for each person. Everyone checked also gets a self reflection.
  • Community tab. Optional. Make a one question link for a partner, kupuna, or volunteer who knows someone's work. Each link works once and stops when the review closes.
  • Setup tab. Press Open the review.

3. Send everyone their link about 10 minutes

  • Links tab. Press Email everyone their link. Anyone without email gets a Copy message button instead. Text them their own message.
  • Each link is personal and signs that phone in as that person. Never forward one person's link to someone else. If someone loses theirs, press New link next to their name.

4. While the review is open two or three weeks

  • Progress tab. Check once or twice a week. It shows who has started and finished, never what anyone wrote.
  • Press Email a nudge to everyone behind, on Progress or on the Cycles page under Today's nudges. Copy the nudge for anyone without email. Five minutes is enough for the quick version.
  • Progress, Early signals. Press Check new answers to see how many sound like burnout, unclear roles, safety concerns, or friction. No names, no quotes. Use it to ask the whole team better questions.
  • You cannot read any answers yet. They open when you close the review, so people can write freely.

5. Close the review 1 minute

  • Setup tab. When everyone is done, or on the close date, press Close the review. Answers lock and the Letters tab opens.

6. Write each person's letter 15 to 30 minutes each

In the Letters tab, open each person and work through three tabs.

  • Raw. Read every answer about them. Answers are shuffled, so you cannot tell who wrote what, except a mahalo someone chose to sign. Play any voice recordings. Every time you open Raw, it is logged. A note at the top says if any of their reviewers tend to write more generously or critically than the group, so you can read with that in mind. It never says which answers are whose.
  • Draft. With AI on, press Write a draft. In about 30 seconds the AI compiles a draft that summarizes what was sent, using people's exact words, measured against your values. Every quote is checked against the answers. With AI off, or if you prefer, press Start from the answers: every answer about them, word for word, ready for you to rewrite.
  • Draft. Now write the final version in your own words. Edit it like any document. To bring in a line, highlight it in Raw and press Add as quote. It saves as you type.
  • Share. Uncheck any section you want to keep to yourself, read the preview, and press Share.

7. Let each person know 1 minute each

  • Share. Press Email and their name. The email has a fresh link to their letter. No email for them? Press Copy message and text it. Once several letters are shared, Letters has an Email everyone whose letter is shared button.
  • When they open it, their home page says Your letter is ready. They read it on their phone and can write a reflection back to you. Their reflection shows up at the bottom of the Share tab.

8. Write the team report about 20 minutes

  • Reports tab. Press Write a draft (or, with AI off, Start from an outline). The AI reads every answer in the review and compiles one report on the whole team: what you do well, where you struggle, what people asked for, and ideas for next year. It never names anyone.
  • Rewrite it in your own words. Then print it, or press Copy for a doc or email to paste it into a Google Doc, Word, or an email.
  • Board report. Lower on the same tab, press Write the board report for one page with no names and no quotes. Edit it, then press Print the board report, or Copy for a doc or email to paste it into a Google Doc, Word, or an email.
  • If you want staff to see it, press Share with everyone in this review. It shows on their home page as What we learned together. Press Email everyone that it is ready.

9. Talk story

  • Talk story tab. Enter the times you are free and press Lay out the meetings. Download the combined calendar file for yourself, and send each person their time or their own file.
  • Draft tab, Talk story prep. Before each meeting, press Write talk story prep for openers, one thing to celebrate, one tension, one question, and goals from last time. Only you see it.
  • Bring their letter (press Print or save as PDF in Share) and their reflection. Talk through the questions at the end of the letter and set two or three goals. They add the goals on My page.

10. Finish

  • Setup tab. When every letter and the team report are done, press Mark published.
  • Settings, Your data. Press Export now to save a full copy for your records: one JSON file and one file per person.

Good to know

  • You can read every answer, but never who wrote it, except a mahalo someone chose to sign. If someone has only one reviewer, you will know whose answers those are.
  • The AI never sees who wrote what. It sees the words, the question, your values, and the name of the person the letter is for.
  • Staff can change their answers any time until you close the review.
  • Someone lost their link? They can type their email or phone number on the home page, and the app emails them a fresh one. Or, in People, press Email link (or Copy link) next to their name. That link works any time of year, even with no review open, for My page and mahalo.
  • Only one review can be open at a time. Once a review closes, its questions, reviewers, and people lock. Reopen it in Setup to change them.
  • On a shared computer or phone, use Sign out at the top when you finish. Staff have their own Sign out too.
  • Voice recordings delete themselves 90 days after they are recorded. The typed words stay.
  • To delete everything from a review, go to its Setup tab and type its name. This cannot be undone, so export first.
  • Someone left? In People, uncheck Active. Their past reviews stay, and new reviews leave them out.
  • All year, staff can thank each other, ask each other for feedback, and keep their goals on My page. Feedback answers and mahalo from the year feed into each person's next letter. Remove anything from the mahalo board in Settings.
