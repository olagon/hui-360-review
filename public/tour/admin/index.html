The same thing, in words

Once, before your first review: add everyone in People (staff, interns, volunteers, board) with their email, set your values, mark your director and any team leads, and in Settings add your logo, choose how email is sent, and turn AI on or off.

1. Set up: start a review. Its tabs walk you through People, Questions, and Reviewers. Press Auto fill, then change anything. Optionally include an upward review of the director, or ask a partner outside the organization one question.

2. Send links: in Links, press Email everyone their link. Each person with Send email checked gets their own link. For anyone without email, copy their message and text it.

3. While it is open: Progress shows status only. Email a nudge to everyone behind in one click. With AI on, early signals show counts of themes like burnout, never names. If you write reviews too, press My reviews at the top of admin.

4. Close it: answers lock and the Letters tab opens. Answers are shuffled, have no names, and every read is logged.

5. Write each letter: read Raw, have AI compile a draft that summarizes what was sent, make it yours, and choose what to share.

With or without AI: AI is optional, one switch in Settings. With it off, everyone types, each letter starts from the answers word for word, reports start from an outline, and nothing is sent to any AI. Everything else works the same.

6. Let them know: in Share, press Email and their name. They get their own link, read the letter on their phone, and can write a reflection back.

7. Talk story: enter when you are free, lay out the meetings, and send calendar files. With AI on, use the prep notes. Set two or three goals together.

8. The whole team: write the team report for staff and the board report for your board. Print them, or copy them into a doc or email.

9. Finish: mark the review published and export a copy from Settings, Your data.
