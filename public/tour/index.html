The same thing, in words

Hui 360 is 100% open source and free under the MIT license. It was inspired by huiMAU in Hāmākua, Hawaiʻi Island.

For staff: answer on your phone, a little at a time. Type, or tap Talk and say it. It works with no signal and nothing is lost. There is an "I only have 5 minutes" mode, and no passwords, just a personal link.

Anonymous by design: answers are stored with who they are about, never who wrote them. Only a mahalo you choose to sign carries your name.

For the director: set up a review in minutes with your own questions and values, auto fill who reviews whom, watch progress, and send gentle nudges.

The letters: AI compiles a draft that summarizes what everyone sent, using exact quotes measured against your values, and every quote is checked word for word. You write the final version and decide what each person sees.

With or without AI: AI is optional, one switch in Settings. With it on, staff can talk instead of type, AI compiles a first draft of each letter that you rewrite, and early signals count themes without names. With it off, everyone types, each letter starts from the answers word for word, and nothing is sent to any AI. Everything else works the same.

Your language: ʻokina and kahakō done right, and Hawaiian quotes stay in Hawaiian. Translate every screen into ʻŌlelo Hawaiʻi in Settings, then offer English, ʻŌlelo Hawaiʻi, or both when the translation is ready.

What it costs: for a team of 20, hosting, database, and voice to text are free on Cloudflare. AI draft summaries cost $0 to $0.25 a review. A typical year is $0 to $1.

Getting started: copy the project from GitHub, create a few free Cloudflare pieces, lock the admin pages to your director's email, add your people, values, and logo, and send each person their link. It takes an afternoon and one person comfortable following instructions in a terminal. Full setup steps.

Your words stay yours: export everything any time, delete a whole review in one step, and every admin read is logged.
