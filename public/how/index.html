How this works

Prefer to watch? See the 1 minute tour

At each review, whether that is once a year or more often, everyone at the Hui looks back on their own work and on the work of a few coworkers. Your director reads it all, writes each person a letter about their work, and sits down with them to talk story. This app is where that happens.

For staff

  1. Open your link. You get a personal link by email, or by text from your director. No password and no app to install. It opens straight to your list. Lost it? Type your email or phone number on the home page and a fresh one comes by email. If that box is not there, ask your director.
  2. See your list. First your own self reflection, then one card for each coworker you are reviewing. Each card shows how far along you are and about how long the rest will take.
  3. Answer one question at a time. Type, or, if your organization uses voice, tap Talk and say it out loud. Your words appear in the box so you can fix anything.
  4. No signal? Keep going. Everything saves on your phone as you type and sends itself when you are back online.
  5. Short on time? Tap I only have 5 minutes to answer just the key questions, then come back for the rest.
  6. Mahalo first. Every coworker review starts with gratitude. You can choose to sign your mahalo so they know it came from you.
  7. Read your letter. After the review closes, you get a letter about your work with what coworkers appreciated and where you can grow. You can write a reflection back.

All year

My page holds your letters, your two or three goals until your next review, mahalo you received, and a place to thank a coworker or ask one for feedback on something specific, like a workday. The Mahalo board shows public thanks from across the team.

Your answers stay anonymous

Your answers are saved with the person they are about, never with your name. Admins read them only after the review closes, and they cannot see who wrote what, except a mahalo you chose to sign. Every time an admin reads answers, it is logged. Read exactly how privacy works.

A review, start to finish

  1. The review opens and everyone gets their link.
  2. You answer over a few weeks, a little at a time if you like.
  3. The review closes and answers lock.
  4. Each person gets a letter about their work, written by your director, sometimes starting from an AI draft, and always edited and approved by a person.
  5. You sit down together to talk story and set goals for what comes next.

For admins

Admins set up each review: the questions, who reviews whom, sending links, watching progress, and writing the letters.

Setting up. Add everyone once in People: staff, interns, volunteers, board members, with their email. Each review then has its own People tab to choose who takes part, plus Questions, Reviewers, and Links, where one button emails each person their link. A banner at the top always says what to do next.

When everyone has answered. Close the review in Setup. Then open the Letters tab and go through each person: read every answer about them, let AI compile a draft that summarizes what was sent, write your final version, and share it. They read it on their phone and can write a reflection back to you. Each letter can also be printed or saved as a PDF. When every letter is shared, mark the review published, and save a copy of everything from Settings, Your data.

With or without AI. AI is optional. With it on, staff can answer by voice and AI compiles a first draft of each letter that you rewrite. With it off, staff type, each letter starts from the answers word for word, and nothing is sent to any AI model. Admins choose in Settings.

Also there for admins. Early signals (counts of themes like burnout, never names, with AI on), one question links for partners outside the organization, an optional review of the director and supervisors, sharing a letter with a person's supervisor when the director chooses, talk story prep for each meeting, a scheduler that makes calendar files, a team report, and a one page board report.

Want to see the end result first? In Settings, load the sample review. It shows a finished letter and a reflection, using made up people.

Admins do not use a magic link. Go to the admin page and enter your email. A one time code arrives by email; type it in and you are signed in for the day. Only emails on the admin list can get in.
